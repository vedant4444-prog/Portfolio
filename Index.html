About

Chicago, IL

I’m an AI product manager with more than four years of experience owning product roadmap strategy, requirements gathering and cross-functional execution.

Today I work at an early-stage AI startup, partnering directly with the CEO to align product priorities with business objectives and to shape the strategic roadmap while the company scales. Before that I spent four years in pharmaceutical commercial and product roles across Bangalore and Raipur, turning market insight and customer feedback into product and go-to-market recommendations.

The thread through all three roles is the same: take a business need that arrives ambiguous, turn it into requirements a team can build against, and come back with a recommendation an executive can act on. I work in SQL, Power BI, Tableau and Excel, and deliver through Agile.
